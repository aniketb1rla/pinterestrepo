A collection of 9 cohesive fintech social media ad creatives for a savings app named Stacko. The designs use a refined color palette of deep forest green, black, and crisp off-white, spotlighting custom emerald green translucent 3D rendered assets like jars, coins, vaults, and percentage signs. The typography pairs prominent bold sans-serif headlines with clean explanatory subtext, maintaining a sleek, trustworthy, and modern financial aesthetic.
