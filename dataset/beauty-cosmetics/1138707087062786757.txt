The Ordinary - Skincare Social Media Carousel | Behance Project. an advertisement for the ordinary skin care company, with images of different products on it. A minimalist and science-backed social media carousel design for Deciem's The Ordinary. This project features two hero products (Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% and Hyaluronic Acid 2% + B5) and breaks down their benefits, key ingredients, and how to use them in a clear, visually engaging format. Designed to educate consumers and reflect the brand's clinical, no-nonsense identity
