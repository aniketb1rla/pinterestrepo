Poster design is a visual communication medium that combines images, typography, and color to deliver messages clearly and attractively. It is created to capture attention, convey information quickly, and leave a strong visual impression. A good poster design balances aesthetics and function, using composition and visual hierarchy to guide the viewer’s focus and effectively communicate its purpose
