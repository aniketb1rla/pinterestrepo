Gravitas is a condensed serif typeface inspired by the Latin word meaning weight, dignity, and substance. It evokes that sense of authority that doesn’t need to shout. Quiet power. In design, this shows up through clean structure and restraint. The condensed proportions create momentum, and the minimal difference in stroke weight creates solidity within each letterform, word, and paragraph.
