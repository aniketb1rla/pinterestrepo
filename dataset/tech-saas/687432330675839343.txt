A mature B2B SaaS product can have real users, years of development, and powerful features—and still lose new users before they see its value. In this short UX case study, I break down a financial desktop app whose onboarding showed technical language, an empty data table, and advanced controls too early. The redesign didn’t remove features. It guided users through one clear flow: upload statements, see the result, confirm accuracy, then explore the professional tools. Watch the full video
