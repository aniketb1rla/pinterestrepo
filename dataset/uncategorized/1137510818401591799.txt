women working on sewing machines in a factory. 🧵 Small Order? Choose the Right Factory.
Small quantity does NOT mean cheap.

• Ask: what's the minimum order?
• Count all costs: sample, fabric, trims, shipping
• Check if the sample looks good and fits right
• If your product sells well, can they produce more?

Choose the wrong factory = lose money + waste time
