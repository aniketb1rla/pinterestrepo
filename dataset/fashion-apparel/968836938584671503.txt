229 eco-friendly garment factories in Bangladesh now: BGMEA. an assembly line with spools of thread on the machine and lights in the background. The number of LEED-certified green factories in Bangladesh is 229 now, after three more apparel and textile factories were recently certified by the US Green Building Council: Sepal Garments Ltd in Gazipur, Unitex Spinning Ltd (Unit-2) in Chattogram and Ananta Huaxiang Limited in Narayanganj. The country is now home to 61 of the top 100 highest-rated LEED certified factories in the world
