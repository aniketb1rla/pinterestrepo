IndustriALL Bangladesh Council has demanded a $260 monthly minimum wage for Bangladesh garment workers, citing higher living costs and market volatility.
IBC said essentials, transport and medical costs had strained workers, alongside a 100 to 142 per cent rise for government employees.
It plans grade-wise wage proposals once the full wage board is formed
