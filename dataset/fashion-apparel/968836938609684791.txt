IAF launches manifesto for smart, sustainable apparel manufacturing. an assembly line with workers working on sewing machines. IAF’s new manifesto urges apparel manufacturers and brands to move beyond lowest-cost sourcing and focus on end-to-end productivity, demand alignment and supply chain resilience. 
It highlights smart flexibility, stronger textile-apparel collaboration and lower inventory risk. 
The roadmap centres on defining shared goals, enabling innovation and standardising scalable industry practices
