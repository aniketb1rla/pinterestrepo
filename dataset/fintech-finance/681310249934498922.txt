Finora — Reimagining Banking App & UI/UX Design. two iphones displaying the same credit card as they are in front of a blue background. Finora is a modern fintech mobile app concept designed around one simple idea: give users a clear view of their money and make financial actions effortless. The experience brings balances, cards, transactions, transfers, payments, offers, and card controls into one clean digital banking ecosystem. The interface combines a minimal dark UI, soft blue gradients, rounded surfaces, and focused interactions to create a premium yet approachable financial experience
