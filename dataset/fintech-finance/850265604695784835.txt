Fluo Fintech Advertising Campaign. an advertisement for fluc showing a ball of yarn tied to the end of a string. This concept project explores how visual metaphors can communicate simplicity in financial technology. Inspired by the challenges faced by young business owners, the campaign uses a rope to symbolize payment friction, gradually transitioning from knots and tension to smooth, flowing motion.

Fluo is presented as the solution that removes this friction, enabling seamless, stress-free payments and smoother business operations.
