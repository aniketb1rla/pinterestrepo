ScaleApp is a budgeting and property portfolio analysis app that helps young Australians navigate housing affordability with confidence. Arounda designed a modular social media system that explains complex financial ideas through minimal, typography-led visuals. We structured Instagram posts, stories, Facebook, and LinkedIn covers as one coherent visual language, using gradients for depth and real interface screens to anchor trust.
