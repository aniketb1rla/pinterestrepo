In the recession of the 90s, Don - along with many others - left what he knew as home, and migrated to Australia to start a new life and contribute to the local textile industry. For the past 34 years Don has been a knitting machine technician, and his knowledge keeps these machines running. Without him, much of this craft would struggle to survive.
