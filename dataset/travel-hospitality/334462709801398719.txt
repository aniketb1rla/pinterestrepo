A three-part print ad campaign for travel company SNCM featuring humorous, low-angle photography of kids leaving home while miniature toys—a green army man, a teddy bear, and a doll—desperately try to hold them back. Each scene is realistically staged and softly lit, paired with a floating white travel ticket graphic in the upper-right corner. The aesthetic is whimsical and cinematic, communicating family summer travel through playful narrative imagery.
